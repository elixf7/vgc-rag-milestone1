# Trick Room Teams

Trick Room reverses the turn order for five turns: the slowest Pokémon moves
first. Teams built around it run intentionally slow, bulky attackers with high
Attack or Special Attack and minimum Speed investment.

The core challenge of a Trick Room team is getting Trick Room up safely. The
setter is usually bulky and may carry a Mental Herb to avoid being Taunted, or a
redirection partner (Follow Me / Rage Powder) to absorb a hit while the room goes
up. Lead the setter alongside a partner that threatens the most likely Taunt or
Fake Out user.

Against Trick Room, your options are: (1) bring your own Trick Room to flip it
back, (2) Taunt the setter before it moves, (3) Fake Out or KO the setter on the
turn it tries to set up, or (4) stall out the five turns with Protect and switches
while keeping fast Pokémon in the back.

Count the turns. Trick Room lasts exactly five turns including the turn it is set.
Track the remaining count; once it is about to expire, your faster Pokémon regain
control and slow attackers become liabilities. Do not over-commit slow Pokémon in
the final Trick Room turn.
