# Protect Baiting and Tempo

Protect is the most-used move in doubles for a reason: it blocks all damage for a
turn, scouts the opponent's plan, stalls weather and Trick Room turns, and lets a
threatened Pokémon survive a double-target. Reading and baiting Protect is a core
skill.

Bait a Protect by representing a kill the opponent must avoid, then doing
something else. For example, if the opponent's key Pokémon is in range of your
attacker, they may Protect it expecting the hit — so instead set up Tailwind,
switch, or KO their other Pokémon while the protected one wastes its turn. A
Pokémon that just Protected usually cannot Protect again successfully (consecutive
Protect has a falling success rate), so the turn after a Protect is often the
safest time to commit your big attack.

Conversely, do not walk into a free Protect. If your only play telegraphs an
obvious KO, the opponent gets a free turn to set up or reposition. Mix in
spread moves (which still chip a Protecting partner's ally), switches, and Fake
Out to keep the opponent guessing.

Track who has Protected recently. Use Protect yourself to stall the final turns
of opposing Tailwind, Trick Room, or weather, flipping the tempo back to your
favor once the timer runs out.
