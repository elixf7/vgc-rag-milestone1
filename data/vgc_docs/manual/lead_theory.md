# Lead Theory in Pokémon Champions Doubles

The lead pair sets the tempo for the entire game. A good lead applies immediate
pressure while keeping your win condition safe in the back. The two most common
lead archetypes are "speed control + offense" and "disruption + setup".

Fake Out users (Rillaboom, Incineroar-style pivots) are premium leads because a
free flinch buys a turn of tempo and can stop an opposing setup or speed-control
move. Pair Fake Out with a hard-hitting partner so the flinched target can be
removed or heavily chipped on turn one.

Lead a speed-control Pokémon (Tailwind setter like a Whirlwind/Tailwind flyer, or
a Trick Room setter) when your team is built around that mode. Do not lead your
win condition into an unknown threat — keep frail sweepers in the back until the
opposing leads are committed.

When unsure, lead the pair that is hardest to punish: bulky Pokémon with
Intimidate or redirection give you the most information with the least risk.
Against an opponent whose Mega user is obvious, consider leading a Pokémon that
pressures it before it Mega Evolves.
