# Terrain Effects

Terrains coat the battlefield for five turns and affect every grounded Pokémon
(those not Flying-type and without Levitate or an air balloon). Each terrain has a
distinct competitive role, and tracking the remaining turns matters as much as it
does for weather and Trick Room.

Grassy Terrain boosts grounded Grass moves by 30%, restores 1/16 HP per turn to
grounded Pokémon, and halves the power of Earthquake and other ground-shaking
moves. It is a staple of Grassy Surge teams (Rillaboom) that pair it with Grassy
Glide, a priority move under the terrain. The passive healing also blunts chip
strategies.

Electric Terrain prevents grounded Pokémon from falling asleep and boosts grounded
Electric moves by 30%. It shuts down Spore and other sleep strategies, which can
single-handedly counter a redirection-plus-Spore game plan.

Psychic Terrain blocks priority moves against grounded Pokémon and boosts Psychic
moves. It is a strong tool against Fake Out and priority-based offense — under
Psychic Terrain your grounded Pokémon cannot be flinched by a priority Fake Out.

Misty Terrain prevents status conditions on grounded Pokémon and halves Dragon
move damage. It is defensive insurance against Thunder Wave, Spore, and Will-O-Wisp.

When two terrains are set, the most recent wins. Account for terrain in your calcs:
a Grassy Glide that normally has priority does nothing special off the terrain, and
an Earthquake hits much harder once Grassy Terrain expires. Plan your big ground
move for the turn the terrain runs out.
