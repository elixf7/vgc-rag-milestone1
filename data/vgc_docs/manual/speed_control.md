# Speed Control: Tailwind, Trick Room, and Paralysis

Speed control decides who moves first, and in doubles moving first usually means
winning the damage race. The three pillars are Tailwind (doubles your side's
Speed for four turns), Trick Room (reverses order for five turns), and disruptive
Speed drops (paralysis halves Speed, Icy Wind / Electroweb lower it).

Tailwind is the most popular mode for offensive teams. A flyer or fast support
Pokémon sets it on turn one, and your attackers clean up while it is active. The
weakness is the four-turn clock: track `tailwind_turns` and make your aggression
count before it expires. Bring a second Tailwind setter against teams that try to
out-stall you.

Trick Room is the opposite: slow, bulky attackers dominate for five turns. The two
modes hard-counter each other, so identify the opponent's speed-control plan early.
If both teams set their mode, the field can flip turn to turn — plan two turns
ahead.

Paralysis and Speed-lowering moves are flexible, partial speed control that does
not expire on a fixed clock. Spreading paralysis with a move like Nuzzle or Thunder
Wave can permanently shift a key matchup. A Choice Scarf gives a single Pokémon a
1.5x Speed boost without setup, often the cleanest way to revenge-kill a faster
threat or to under-speed for Trick Room if you choose not to invest.
