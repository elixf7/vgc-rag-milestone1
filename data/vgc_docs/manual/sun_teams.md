# Sun Teams and Weather Control

Sun teams use harsh sunlight to power up Fire-type moves (1.5x), enable
Chlorophyll sweepers (doubled Speed in sun), and weaken incoming Water attacks.
A sun setter (Drought ability or a Sunny Day user) leads alongside an abuser that
immediately benefits.

Chlorophyll sweepers double their Speed under sun, often outrunning the entire
opposing field including Choice Scarf users. The danger is that sun is fragile:
if the opponent overrides your weather (rain, sand, snow) your Chlorophyll
attacker drops back to normal Speed and may be outsped and KO'd.

Weather wars matter. Whoever sets weather last controls the field. Bring a backup
weather setter or a Pokémon that does not depend on weather so a single weather
swap does not collapse your game plan. Track `weather_turns_remaining`; ability
weather (Drought) lasts five turns and can be refreshed by switching the setter
back in.

Against sun, deny the speed boost: change the weather, use priority moves to
bypass the doubled Speed, or Trick Room the Chlorophyll sweeper so its Speed works
against it.
