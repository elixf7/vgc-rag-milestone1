# Reading the Opposing Mega Evolution

In Regulation M-A, Mega Evolution is the only battle gimmick, and each team may
Mega Evolve exactly once per battle. Identifying the opponent's likely Mega user
during team preview is one of the most valuable reads you can make.

A Pokémon is the probable Mega user if: it has a strong Mega form in the current
meta, it appears alongside teammates that support a setup sweep (redirection,
Tailwind, Fake Out), and it is the team's clearest win condition. Common Champions
Mega threats include Mega Feraligatr (gains Dragonize), Mega Clefable (Magic
Bounce), Mega Garchomp, and Mega Emboar (Mold Breaker, which bypasses abilities
like Levitate or Lightning Rod).

Play around the Mega before it activates. If you expect a Mega Feraligatr, keep a
Fairy or bulky Dragon-resistant answer healthy. Because the Mega is once-per-game,
baiting the opponent into Mega Evolving on a turn where it gets little value (e.g.
into a Protect or a switch) is a major tempo win.

Track `mega_used` for both sides. Once the opponent has spent their Mega, their
remaining Pokémon are locked into base forms, which often shifts the speed tiers
and damage math in your favor. Plan your own Mega timing to answer theirs.
