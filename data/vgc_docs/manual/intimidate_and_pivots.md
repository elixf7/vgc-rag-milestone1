# Intimidate, Pivots, and Defensive Positioning

Intimidate lowers the Attack of both opposing Pokémon by one stage when its user
switches in or leads. It is one of the strongest defensive tools in doubles
because it blunts physical offense for free and can flip a guaranteed KO into a
survivable hit. Pokémon with Intimidate make excellent leads and mid-game pivots.

Repeated Intimidate (switching the user in and out) can stack Attack drops to -2
or lower, neutering a physical attacker entirely. Opponents answer Intimidate with
Defiant or Competitive (which sharply raise an offensive stat when lowered), with
special attackers that ignore Attack drops, or by using Clear Amulet / White Herb
to negate the drop. Read whether the opponent's Attack drop actually helped them.

Pivoting moves (U-turn, Volt Switch, Flip Turn, Parting Shot) let you deal damage
or apply a debuff and then bring in a better matchup on the same turn. Good
pivoting preserves momentum: you answer the opponent's threat while repositioning
your win condition. The risk is predictability — a pivot into an obvious answer
can be punished by a double-target or a switch of their own.

Defensive positioning is about keeping the right Pokémon healthy. Sack a fainted
or non-essential Pokémon to preserve your win condition, use Protect to deny a
double-target, and switch to absorb a predicted attack with a resist. Track HP
percentages and status: a burned physical attacker or a paralyzed sweeper is far
less threatening, and that should change how aggressively you play around it.
