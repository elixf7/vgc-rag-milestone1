# Common Team Archetypes

Recognizing the opposing team's archetype during team preview tells you what they
want to do and how to disrupt it. Most VGC teams fall into a handful of broad
modes, and the right counter-strategy follows from the archetype.

Hyper Offense / Tailwind: fast attackers backed by a Tailwind setter race to
overwhelm you in four turns. They are fragile. Counter by setting your own
Tailwind, using priority, surviving the initial burst with bulk and Intimidate,
then cleaning up once their Tailwind expires.

Trick Room: slow, bulky attackers that dominate for five turns under reversed
order. Counter by Taunting or KO-ing the setter, bringing your own Trick Room to
flip it, or stalling the five turns with Protect and fast switches.

Balance / Bulky Offense: a mix of offensive and defensive Pokémon that play for
positioning and grind out advantages with Intimidate, redirection, and chip. These
games are decided by reads and resource management rather than a single burst.
Preserve your win condition and out-position them.

Weather (Sun / Rain / Sand / Snow): a weather setter enables abusers (Chlorophyll,
Swift Swim, Sand Rush) or powers up moves. Counter by changing or removing the
weather, which collapses the abuser's speed or power advantage.

Redirection + Setup: a Follow Me / Rage Powder user shields a setup sweeper for a
free turn. Counter with spread moves that ignore redirection, Fake Out on the
redirector, or a Grass-type / Safety Goggles against powder moves.

The key skill is mapping six Pokémon to an archetype quickly, identifying their win
condition and speed-control mode, and planning a lead that disrupts the plan before
it starts.
